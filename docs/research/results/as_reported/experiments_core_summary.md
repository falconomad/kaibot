How to read it: fill `base` = decided after the close of T, bought and sold at the close of T+1 at half a spread a side plus impact; `stress` = the open of T+1 with spread and impact doubled. Costs `2x` / `3x` multiply spread and impact of cost model v1 (regulatory fees stay). Break-even is the multiple of spread and impact at which net profit is zero, from the 0x and 1x runs of that row's fill, cap and earnings version (pre-registered); the report JSON also keeps the multiple with the 1x trades held fixed as a cross-check. Sharpe is annualised from daily mark-to-market returns (no risk-free rate); the 50% haircut is beside it; a backtested Sharpe above about 1.0 is a warning sign. Max drawdown is mark-to-market at the close. Turnover is (bought + sold) / 2 a year over the average equity; cost is spread, impact and fees a year in bp of the average equity. Trades are closed positions (resize sales are not counted). The percentile columns say where the rule's net Sharpe and CAGR fall among the matched random entries (same dates, entries per date, sizing and holding periods; random stocks of the same universe and liquidity filter).
