# Name check: "Kaibo"

Issue #255 · 16 Nov 2026 · Design. A quick look by web search for companies and apps already called Kaibo, mainly in
finance and software.

**What this is:** facts found on the day, with links. **What it is not:** a trademark search, a domain check or legal
advice. No conclusion is drawn here about whether the name can be used. Not finding something does not mean it does not
exist.

## How it was done

About a dozen web searches for "Kaibo" with words such as app, fintech, trading, software, AI, trademark, company,
plus the App Store and Google Play. Where a page could be opened, the facts below come from the page. Where only the
search result's title was seen, the entry says "title only".

The two Japanese words are spelled with a long o (*kaibō*), which "kaibo" resembles but does not match exactly.

## Not found in these searches

## Not checked

- The trademark registers themselves (for example USPTO, EUIPO, DPMA, WIPO), in any class.
- Domain names and social handles.
- Company registers.
- Languages other than those in the table.

## For Product

Before the name is used in public beyond the app's own UI, a proper clearance search by someone qualified is the next
step. The entries above are the ones to hand over: the two investment firms that share the name, the audio brand with
its apps, the two US filings, and the abandoned KAIBOT application.
