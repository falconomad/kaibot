Issue #296. The one reference for how the app looks and moves. It replaces the v3 "night" design-system board
(`docs/design/v3/project/v3/DesignSystem.dc.html`, issue #79).

  for the light theme, and it is responsive: under 761 px it shows the phone layout.

## Spacing, shape, sizes

- **Spacing:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 px. One unit is the logo's stem, 4 px.
- **Corners:** 8 (tag), 12 (input), 16 (row), 24 (card, sheet), pill (buttons, chips).
- **Heights:** 44 px (tap target, minimum), 48 px (tab bar item), 64 px (list row), 52 px (primary button), 48 px (form field), 22 px (tag).
- **Stroke:** `--stroke` 2px, round caps and joins. **The point:** `--point` 8px, 10 px at the end of the chart line, 6 px above the active tab.
- **Focus:** 2 px `--focus` ring, 2 px offset, on every control.

## The glow

One layer fixed to the screen: a radial glow of `--sea-tint` fading into `--paper`, 110% by 70% of the screen, with a faint
noise. Sign-in bottom left, Home top right, small offsets for the other tabs. Cards, rows and sheets stay opaque on it.

## Components and their states

Shown at phone and desktop width on the page. Where a cell comes from: **rule** is written in a design document;
**as built** is how the app does it today with no written rule; **follows** is this page applying a written rule to a
case no document names. The last two need Design's sign-off. The company tile was "as built" until it was signed
off in #307; its rule is in `brand_system_v1.md`, section 5.

| Component | Default | Loading | Empty | Error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Card** | Surface, 1 px hairline, 24 px corners, no shadow. Padding 16 on phones, 24 on desktop. *(rule)* | Skeleton rows with the height of the content they stand for, so nothing jumps. *(rule)* | One plain sentence that says what to expect next. No picture. *(as built)* | Says what could not load, that nothing in the engine changed, the server's message, and a "Try again" button. *(as built)* |
| **Buttons** | Pill, 52 px. Primary: blue fill, one per screen or sheet. Secondary: raised fill, control border. Destructive: red label, red edge, never blue. *(rule)* | The button is disabled and its label says what is happening ("Saving…"). No spinner. *(as built)* | Disabled: 45% opacity, plus the reason in words nearby. *(rule)* | The button returns to its resting state; the message stands beside it in words, as a form error does. *(follows)* |
| **Tabs** | Resting: third-level colour. Active: text colour plus the 6 px blue point above the icon. Two cues, neither colour alone. *(rule)* | The bar does not move or change while a page loads. *(rule)* | The bar does not change when a page is empty. *(follows)* | The Engine tab carries a red dot while a halt is in force and an amber dot when the engine is offline. *(rule)* |
| **Chips** | Pill, 44 px, control border. Selected: text-colour fill, page-colour label. Not blue. *(rule)* | A ticker being checked: dashed edge (dashed means provisional) and the word "Checking". *(as built)* | No chips: one plain sentence in their place. *(as built)* | A refused ticker: red tint, red edge, the symbol struck through, and the reason in words. *(as built)* |
| **Logo** | The wordmark alone in the header and on sign-in; the k alone where space is short. The files are used unchanged. *(rule)* | Once per app load the mark draws itself in under a second. With reduce motion it shows finished. *(rule)* | None. The mark is the same on every screen. *(follows)* | None. The mark is never recoloured to carry a state. *(follows)* |
| **Company tile** | A squircle with the company's logo on a colour sampled from that logo; grey 50 when none was sampled. Drawn 8% larger than the size it is given. *(rule)* | The tile is laid out at its size first, on its colour (grey 50 until sampled); the logo fades in over 200 ms. *(rule)* | No logo: the ticker's initials on one of ten dark tints, never under 12 px. *(rule)* | The logo failed to load: the initials tile. A row with no company (a halt): a neutral tile with a red icon. *(rule)* |
| **Skeleton** | Raised-surface bars (`--surface-2`), row-shaped, 8 px corners, an opacity breathe: 1 to 0.55 and back every 1.6 s. No blue. *(rule)* | It is the loading state. It may loop, because it carries no data. With reduce motion the bars stand still. *(rule)* | Never left running: the empty sentence takes its place. *(follows)* | Never left running: the error message takes its place. *(follows)* |

## Logo

The Rising k; the wordmark alone in the header, on sign-in and on New run. The files are used unchanged.

**Clear space:** the files carry their own margin: 14 units on every side of the wordmark, where the stem is 15 units
wide and the letters 88 tall, so about one stem (`logo/gen/build.py`, `pad=14`). Keep text, edges and other marks outside
it. No document states a clear-space rule or a smallest size in words yet (a finding for Design).

## Company tile

A squircle (superellipse, n = 5) with the company's logo on a colour sampled from that logo (grey 50 when none was
sampled), or the ticker's initials; a hairline, white at 8%, on the same curve. Sizes given: 32, 36, 40 and 52; each is
drawn 8% larger (35, 39, 43, 56 px; #293, stakeholder, 17 Nov 2026). Initials: three or four letters, bold, never under
12 px, on one of ten dark tints. Signed off as built (#307; `apps/web/src/components/StockBadge.tsx`); the rule is in
`brand_system_v1.md`, section 5.

## Motion

| | |
|---|---|
| **Things rise** | Sheets and rows: 320 ms in, 240 ms out (`--d-sheet`). Height never animates. |
| **Lines draw** | `--d-draw` 300ms: the chart line on first load, as the logo's arm does. |
| **The point pops** | `--d-point` 180ms with `--ease-pop` cubic-bezier(.3, 1.4, .5, 1); it pulses once when a new value arrives. |
| **Easing** | `cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)` for everything entering; `cubic-bezier(.4, 0, 1, 1)` for everything leaving. |
| **Press and select** | 120 ms (`--d-fast`). Fades, colour changes and the number tick: 200 ms (`--d-base`). |
| **Tab change** | Content cross-fades in 200 ms with a 4 px rise. The top bar and the tab bar do not move. |
| **The glow** | Drifts with a transform only: 900 ms from sign-in to Home, 600 ms between tabs, the house easing. |
| **The logo** | Once per app load, under a second: the stem rises, the leg sets, the arm draws, the point appears with one soft pulse. |
| **What may move** | Only `transform` and `opacity`. |
| **No data motion loops** | Anything that shows data plays once: a chart draws once, a point pops once, a number ticks once. Nothing else loops except the two signals below. |
| **Loop: skeletons breathe** | Opacity 1 to 0.55 and back every 1.6 s (`kb-breathe`). No shimmer: it animates `background-position`, which repaints every frame. |
| **Loop: the live dot pulses** | A ring grows from the dot and fades every 2 s (`kb-pulse`), only while the engine is live. |
| **Reduce motion** | No draw, no pop, no pulse; values just change; the glow jumps; the finished logo shows at once. Both loops stop: skeletons stand still and the live dot shows without its ring. |

## Contrast: findings

Ratios are WCAG 2, from `contrast_v1.md`. Text needs 4.5:1; large text and parts of controls need 3:1. Nothing is
changed here.

- **No measured pair is below WCAG AA.** `contrast_v1.md` holds 96 pairs (48 dark, 48 light); every one meets its line.
- **Narrow passes** (within 10% of the line; do not darken or lighten these without measuring): chart: loss mark on a card, dark, 3.01:1 of 3:1; primary button: label on blue, light, 4.72:1 of 4.5:1; third-level text on a raised surface, light, 4.78:1 of 4.5:1.
- **Not in `contrast_v1.md`:** text standing on the glow (`brand_system_v1.md` section 8 says it passes at the brightest point, without a number) and the initials on the company tile's ten tints. Measured for this page: text on the glow's brightest point 15.09:1; second-level text on the glow's brightest point 9.49:1; third-level text on the glow's brightest point 7.30:1; initials on the lightest of the ten tile tints 10.54:1. None is below its line. They should become rows of the file.
- **Not measured anywhere:** the tile's hairline (white at 8%), a company's own logo on its own sampled colour, and disabled controls at 45% opacity (WCAG does not set a minimum for inactive controls). The hairline `--rule` is decoration by rule and nothing relies on it.
- **The logo's blue on a light page:** `#4F95F0` on `#F4F5F7` is 2.79:1 (`logo_concepts.md`), below 3:1. That is why the light files use `#2F6FDE` (4.33:1). Never place the dark-background logo on a light surface.

Measured for this page with `gen/colour.py`; **not** in `contrast_v1.md`:

| What | Colour | On | Ratio | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Text on the glow's brightest point | `#F4F5F7` | `#0F1F39` | 15.09:1 | 4.5:1 |
| Second-level text on the glow's brightest point | `#C0C5CD` | `#0F1F39` | 9.49:1 | 4.5:1 |
| Third-level text on the glow's brightest point | `#A7ADB8` | `#0F1F39` | 7.30:1 | 4.5:1 |
| Initials on the lightest of the ten tile tints | `#F4F5F7` | `#1E3A5F` | 10.54:1 | 4.5:1 |
